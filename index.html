Your questions

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your current number and phone. Your line forwards calls you can't pick up to a number we set up. There is nothing to install.

How do you set it up?

We do the setup after a 15-minute call, plus a few steps in your carrier's forwarding settings that we walk you through. You then make a test call from your own phone before it goes live.

What if I already have an answering service?

It works alongside it. The text-back covers calls your service or receptionist doesn't catch, like after hours and storm days. Callers still reach a person when someone is available.

Is texting callers allowed?

They called you first. The first text includes Reply STOP, and we honor it instantly. Each caller is texted once, and anonymous callers are ignored.

What does it cost to run?

The texting costs are included in the $249 a month. There are no per-message charges to you.

Also included: the estimate form

The estimate request form with photo upload on your website is part of the plan at no extra cost.

Who is this not a fit for?

If you rarely miss calls, this won't help much. It answers the calls you already miss; it doesn't create new ones. We'll tell you on the call if that's you.

Will this guarantee more jobs?

No. We make sure a caller you missed hears back within seconds. We can't guarantee leads, jobs, or revenue. That depends on your market, your pricing, and how people find you.

What if I cancel or need to end my service?

The plan runs 12 months from your first payment, and ending early means owing the remainder. After 12 months you can cancel anytime.

How does payment work?

You pay nothing until it's live. Then $249 a month, billed on the same day each month by card or bank transfer.
