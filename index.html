FAQ

Your questions

What do you actually do?

We find where your roofing business loses money. Then we fix that spot. It may be missed calls, slow follow-up, weak estimate requests or your website.

How do you price it?

Plans start at $249 a month. That covers the missed-call and estimate system. Bigger fixes are priced per job, and Caleb quotes you on the call.

Do I have to change my phone number or my website?

No. You keep your number and your site. Calls you can't pick up forward to a number Caleb sets up. Site changes are only made with your OK.

Is texting callers allowed?

They called you first. The text says Reply STOP, and it stops at once. Each caller is texted once. Anonymous callers are skipped.

What if I cancel?

Monthly plans run 12 months from your first payment. If you end early, you owe the rest. After 12 months, you can cancel any time.

Who is this not for?

If you rarely miss calls and your leads get a fast reply, this won't help much. Caleb will say so on the call.

Will this guarantee more jobs?

No. We fix weak spots so fewer leads slip away. Jobs depend on your market, your price, and how people find you.

How do you get started?

After a 15-minute call, Caleb finds the biggest leak. He sets up the fix and walks you through any steps. You make a test, then it goes live.
