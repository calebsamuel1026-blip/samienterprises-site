We find where your money slips out

You run the jobs. We are a consulting agency that finds where small service businesses lose money, and we fix it.

Sami Enterprises LLC is a revenue consulting agency for owner-led home-service businesses in the United States, including roofing, HVAC, plumbing and electrical companies. We audit where a business loses money, from missed calls and slow follow-up to pricing and late invoices, then set up and run the fixes. Engagements are month to month, and nothing is billed until the work is live.

What we do

We audit your business, find the gaps where money slips away and close them. That could be missed calls, quotes that go cold, prices set too low or invoices paid late. Our team sets up the fix, keeps it running and shows you the numbers every month.

Our principles

  • Honest numbers

    You see the same numbers we see, every month. If something isn't working, we tell you.

  • Pay once it's live

    You pay nothing until it's live and you've approved a test. Then it's month to month.

  • Plain words

    You get straight answers in plain words, and short reports you can read in minutes.

  • You keep your tools

    You keep your phone number, your website and your tools. We work with what you have.

Who we work with

We focus on home-service trades like roofing, HVAC and plumbing, plus other small businesses. We've also worked with a school and a logistics company.

Who we're not for

If you rarely miss calls, chase every quote and get paid on time, we may not help much. We'll tell you that on the call.
